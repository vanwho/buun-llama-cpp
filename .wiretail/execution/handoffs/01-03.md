# 01-03 handoff

## Outcome

Added pure admission arithmetic to the cache budget contract. It charges rounded
weights/fixed state, full-length Turbo4 MTP, graph/dequant scratch,
routing/staging, and headroom in order, then floors remaining capacity to whole
256-token pages. MTP sizing uses the reference 262,144 × 2,048 × 4.125
bits/value geometry (264 MiB before allocator rounding), rejects non-Turbo4 or
missing-scratch inputs, and fails closed on overflow or insufficient capacity.
Diagnostic page limits can only reduce the result.

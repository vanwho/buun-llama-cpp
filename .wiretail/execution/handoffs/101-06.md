  source, destination, and owner dependencies in the graph. Negative indices
  preserve committed rows during rejected provisional updates.
- CUDA copies execute on the backend stream without a per-row host fence.
  CPU support is byte storage only; it is not TurboQuant inference.
- K and V pass through their existing named cache domains, preserving the
  native encoded bytes and per-head padding.

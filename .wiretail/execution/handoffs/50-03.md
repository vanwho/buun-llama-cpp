# Handoff 50-03 — asynchronous selector ownership and page movement

## Result

Implemented the bounded asynchronous selector readback path and preserved the
existing residency transaction as the publication boundary.

- The two mailbox slots can use pager-owned pinned host buffers. CUDA selector
  IDs are copied with `ggml_backend_tensor_get_async`, guarded by a recorded
  CUDA event, and decoded only after a nonblocking event query completes.
- Routing descriptors are frozen per submission and retained until their event
  retires. Pending results leave the prior authenticated selection in force;
  stale identity, content, generation, and rollback evidence is revalidated at
  admission. Failed/cancelled completion also releases graph-owned descriptors.
- Selector overflow is bounded by the two-slot/128-record mailbox. Stable
  boundaries do not wait per layer/token or perform a full Q/catalogue
  readback. The old direct selector readback count is `1`; the working tree
  count is `0`, with one async selector get at `src/llama-kv-cache.cpp:2705`.
- Residency H2D publication remains on the existing bounded upload ring and
  event-backed transaction, so the old mapping remains usable until the new
  transfer commits. Pager teardown cancels owned selector events before pinned
  buffers are freed.

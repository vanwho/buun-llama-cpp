speculative suffix with `all_pinned` after target verification. A transient
sequence removal now releases the completed write frontier before its
generation-checked pager mutation, while direct pager mutations still reject
pages pinned by in-flight work.

and position-shift edits advance page generations, invalidate dirty/changed
host identities, and invalidate both resident-backed and host-only catalog

only by the post-synchronization `seal_ready_pages()` boundary, preserving
in-flight graph protection while allowing exact-page host sealing.

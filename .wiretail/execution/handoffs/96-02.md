## Decisions and invariants

- Summary format version is 6. Mean-K is the arithmetic mean of decoded transformed K over valid dense rows; sparse Mean-K input is rejected.
- Both scorers retain identical eligibility, page/content/codec/transform versions, native-position checks, stable tie ordering, and resident/cold identity. Mean-K does not use recency or residency as relevance.
- Summary catalogue uses three F16 planes; page-update computes the resident mean on CPU/CUDA, and cold-page copy-through preserves it. Cache admission accounting includes the third plane.
- The selector stays at mode 1 based on repeated recall gain and measured cost. Task 100-04 owns final live promotion/replay verification; no server/model/live test was run here.

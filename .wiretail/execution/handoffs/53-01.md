## Decision

The before discriminator was the exact
`L262144/H30208/B128/U128` native-MTP request reaching
`kv_dequant_scratch()` and aborting while growing a 512 MiB side. The repaired
path identifies the native MTP static Turbo4 cache's full `262144`-row view,
charges K and V independently, and returns a recoverable allocation failure
before graph submission when the device cannot fit both sides.

## Telemetry and failed hypotheses

The current CUDA binary accepts the selective CLI and serves the requests, but
the request records and `/metrics` contain no `kv_pager_*` fields. Consequently
page admission/capacity, H/L/attention rows, graph/scratch, faults, prefetch,
evictions, H2D/D2H useful/actual bytes, bandwidth, queue/copy/wait time,
overlap, table rebuilds, and per-trial CPU/RAM/VRAM are recorded as unavailable
in the manifest, never as zero or inferred values. The non-streaming runner
also cannot provide TTFT or inter-token tails; prompt/decode p50/p95/p99 and
raw HTTP timings are retained.

Failed hypotheses retained in the manifest and raw logs are:

1. Native GPU MTP is startable at the model-maximum context: startup OOM.
2. The selective live boundary publishes the pager ledger and runtime
   counters: telemetry is not configured.
3. Selective paging exceeds ordinary CPU-KV by the required floor: matched
   ratio is 1.058x.
4. Exact mode is healthy at the reduced context: all ten requests return 500.

The earlier 32-token selective diagnostic is retained at
`reduced-32768-selective-warm-focus` but is explicitly superseded and not used
in the authoritative ratio.

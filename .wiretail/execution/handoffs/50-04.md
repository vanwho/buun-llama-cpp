CUDA durations are reported as stage/counter observations and are not added as
serial wall time.

  35,322.326 ms; the all-GPU control was 35,403.413 ms prompt evaluation.
- Mature packed prefill was used 194 times. Packed storage was 315,752,000
  bytes, packed workspace 631,505,000 bytes, and packed dequant 76,546,000
  bytes. Scratch high water was 6,358 rows / 32,690,200 bytes.
- Graph counters ended at 344 captures/rebuilds, 144 replays, 488
  submissions/completions, and 811 us logical graph-construction time.
- The largest host-side accounting counters were 3,072 summary builds over
  415,236,000 bytes and 4,608 host-seal D2H calls over 207,618,000 bytes;
  those are the next concrete measurement targets.
- Queue/wait counters ended at 20,253,500 us / 779,194 us, with zero late
  waits and zero stale transfer completions. Packed copy counters ended at
  2,751,220 rows, 60,664 updates, and 27,096 reuses.

The next tuning knobs are summary-build/read reuse and host-seal batching,
followed by packed copy row-update cost. The current append ownership boundary
should remain intact while measuring those categories.

# 31-01 handoff — repaired

Status: done.

## Root cause and fix

The continuation fault was a CUDA stream-ordering/lifetime defect in direct
paged-attention metadata publication. `llm_graph_input_attn_kv::set_input`
used the synchronous tensor setter for page tables, native-position/mask,
query-position, and exact-wave control data. The CUDA buffer setter enqueues
its host-to-device copy on `cudaStreamPerThread`, while the graph consumes the
same buffers on the scheduler's CUDA backend stream. A tail-growing append can
therefore rebuild or replay a graph while the consumer sees stale or partially
updated graph-owned metadata. The fix records the direct CUDA backend on the
graph input and routes all direct metadata uploads through
`ggml_backend_tensor_set_async(direct_backend, ...)`, preserving ordering on
the consuming backend stream. `llama_context::memory_update` also fences the
scheduler before a residency update may reset graph-owned state.

The regression fixture exercises production-sized direct metadata geometry,
tail growth across page boundaries, rollback of an invalid append, and reuse
of a released slot. It keeps split/page-mass metadata enabled and checks finite
output after the asynchronous append sequence.

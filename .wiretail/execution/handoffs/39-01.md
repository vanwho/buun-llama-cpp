## Result

Repaired the current-Q selector handoff at the graph-result boundary. The
selector output is now a graph output, so its compact page IDs remain owned by
the graph allocator until the scheduler fence where the pager reads them.

page-mass outputs; only the explicit direct page-mass producer enters telemetry
publication. This keeps the attention counters and drop reasons truthful while
preserving the existing Turbo4 packed route and mailbox ownership.

# 20-02 handoff — per-layer attention retention and capacity-relative policy

## Outcome

Implemented attention-informed retention as a separate signal from routing
retrieval. Completed page-mass telemetry is published before policy selection;
EMA, peak, reuse/frequency, sample count, and last observation are retained
with layer identity and preserved for authenticated clean host pages. Unknown
cold pages remain distinct from measured zero-attention pages and can still be
recalled through the query-driven routing boundary.

Policy selection now defines writable/speculative/in-flight, caller/anchor,
and sink pins first, then applies capacity-relative recent/history/transient
choices with fallback and deterministic tie behavior. Recent pages are not
implicitly mandatory, so small capacities and pin overflow remain bounded.
The existing CUDA paged-attention reduction is exercised for global final
normalization across split partitions, including valid tails and masks.

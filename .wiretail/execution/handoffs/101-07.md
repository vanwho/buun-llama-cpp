## Decisions and invariants

- Keep the scheduler-wide fence: it settles submitted graph/KV writes, deferred
  VBR target commits, and scheduler-owned host reads. Capture starts after that
  owner boundary; the host worker overlaps its event-driven D2H with later
  compute. No fence protecting graph storage or MTP reads was removed.
- Keep exact-version source pinning through copy completion, bounded staging
  and descriptor queues, nonblocking completion polling, stale-version
  rejection, and waits on queue exhaustion or required slot reuse only.
- Current page/slot is mutation-owned. Maintenance queue allocation failure is
  the only reason readiness checks fall back to a full scan.
- Historical membership and promotion remain at the query boundary. The CUDA
  generation fixture observes zero H2D submissions and zero selector traces
  during seven generated pages, then verifies successful later query promotion.

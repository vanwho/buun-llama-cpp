Implemented the generation-safe `GGML_OP_KV_PAGE_SELECT` catalogue selector and
the fixed whole-page Turbo4 min/max catalogue layout. The selector publishes
resident results first, cold results second, and `-1` for unavailable slots.
Eligibility checks membership, valid length, sequence/page/snapshot generations,
position bounds, and refresh enablement before scoring.

## Findings

The synthetic selector covers negative coefficients, grouped multi-KV heads,
resident/cold partitioning, more logical pages than the resident budget,
future/tail/stale exclusion, and disabled-refresh padding. CUDA source compiles
for the available RTX 4080. The ranking kernel uses one CTA per membership
region so rank dependencies are synchronized deterministically.

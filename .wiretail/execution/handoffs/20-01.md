# 20-01 handoff — all-page query-driven retrieval

## Outcome

Implemented the complete logical inventory boundary for routing. Authenticated
host-only pages (`physical_slot == UINT32_MAX`, `host_clean`, `host_valid`) are
legal candidates; clean GPU eviction retains their summaries, while changed
full page identity invalidates them. Summary tables are indexed by runtime
attention ordinal/KV head and carry a coordinate identity for the positioned
Turbo4 Q/K domain.

The graph boundary records post-positioned `Qcur` tensors per attention layer.
After the existing scheduler fence, the cache reads the actual query, scores
the matching per-layer table over the complete sequence inventory, unions the
bounded candidates, and sends selected cold pages through the existing pager
transfer/live-policy transaction. A query-less boundary now fails closed
instead of fabricating an LRU/recent selection.

## Deferred verification

The selected-off C6144 context control was attempted with the same bundle. Its
first bounded run timed out before a valid response; one distinct retry with a
300-second prefill watchdog later exposed a CUDA illegal-access abort in
`ggml_backend_cuda_synchronize` during `server_slot::capture_mtp_state_diagnostic`.
Both rows are excluded and recorded in
`85-16-context-control-failure.txt`. The all-GPU-native and CPU-main-native
C6144 controls were not run after that abort, so no context-control ratios are
claimed. The final service was restarted on selected-native and `/health`
returned `{"status":"ok"}`. No hardware or human verification was otherwise
deferred.

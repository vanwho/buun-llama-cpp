# 13-01 handoff — reproducible live profiles and performance budgets

## Outcome

Frozen the phase-13 calibration shapes, measurement contract, ranked cost
budget, and three evidence-driven optimization hypotheses in
[`PERFORMANCE_PROFILE.json`](../evidence/PERFORMANCE_PROFILE.json) and its
human-readable companion. No kernel, transfer, server, or policy optimization
was made in this task.

The current Release CUDA configuration is `GGML_CUDA=ON`, CUDA graphs enabled,
`89-real`, `-O3 -DNDEBUG`, with an unstripped server/library retaining
`.symtab`. The corrected same-build controls provide four checkpoints, each
with 15 measured requests (three prompts × five trials), zero errors, and raw
records plus hashes. The bounded direct CUDA fixture provides five measured
CUDA-event samples for four query heads, one query, 529 selected rows, and
three physical pages (20.952 ms median).

## Invariants/evidence

- Calibration records runtime `H`, selected pages/rows, gaps, tails, fault
  batches, and coalesced runs; it does not introduce a fixed hot capacity.
- The 304-page / 77,824-token and 17-token-tail values remain measured or
  fixture geometry, not production defaults.
- Raw HTTP, prompt, and decode timings are accounted in the external records.
  Graph, descriptor, mass reduction, policy, D2H/H2D, waits, sealing, and
  recurrent timing are explicitly unavailable/unattributed because the
  available controls do not expose a live selective pager route.
- The profile ranks direct attention first with medium confidence from the
  bounded fixture; all other rankings are provisional pending live component
  counters.
- Three hypotheses have numeric next-task budgets and boundary regression
  tests: metadata/run coalescing, validated-geometry kernel specialization,
  and bounded pinned/batched transfers.

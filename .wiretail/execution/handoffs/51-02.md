`/srv/ai/paged-kv/results/v10/51-02/20260914T-matched-q0q1q2/` record both
required proofs. The authoritative selective, CPU-main-KV/GPU-draft, and
all-GPU-main+draft matrices each contain 9 passing rows, for 27 measured rows
with exact 6144-token prefill occupancy. The repaired aggregator uses each
question's own raw timings and reports medians, ranges, MTP attempts/accepts,
and exact per-question ratios; it makes no 3x/5x speedup claim.

was 0/251 on every row; all-GPU retry acceptance was also 0/251 on every row.

CUDA illegal-memory-access fault after context-checkpoint creation. The bounded
retry passed after explicitly disabling context checkpoints, RAM cache, and
idle cache slots. This diagnostic is not counted in the authoritative matrix.

256 token continuations. They observed prompt counts 6319 and 6407 and
`append_cache_n=6271` in both cases. The receipt reports this observation
without converting it into a cache-hit percentage.

The final selected successful candidate remains loaded at `127.0.0.1:18080`.

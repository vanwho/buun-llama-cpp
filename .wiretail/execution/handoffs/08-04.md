## Schema and gates

`tools/server/bench/pager_benchmark_contract.py` defines schema 2, canonical
hashing, corpus validation, and fail-closed acceptance-manifest validation.
Schema 1 remains parseable and is labeled legacy/non-acceptance. Unknown
schemas, altered prompts/answers, wrong model/tokenizer hashes, missing MTP
placement, missing reconciled pager ledger, incomplete timing, missing raw
requests, or missing telemetry are rejected for the applicable run.

Frozen quality tolerances are output `1e-5`, logits `1e-4`, routing recall and
captured attention mass `0.95`, held-out task score `1.0`, perplexity relative
delta `2%`, and KL `0.01`. The performance contract is 10 measured trials (5
minimum for checkpoint work), 2 warmups, 3x median warm-focus decode over
same-context ordinary CPU K/V, explicit 5x reporting, 70% of the fresh safe
all-GPU control, steady fault-free p95/median inter-token latency at most 1.5,
and observe overhead at most 5%. Fault segments are reported separately and
exact mode is the quality oracle, excluded from the selective speed gate.

## Operator restoration procedure

Before a live run, record the active profile, health of 8080 and 8091, and the
running service snapshot. Keep `ai-long-memory.service` supplying 8091; never
touch 8092. Use the established
`/srv/ai/benchmarks/run-profile-benchmark.sh` controlled activation path through
the pager adapter. On success, interruption, or failure, restore the captured
profile with `activate-ai-profile.sh`, restart the configured companion service
if needed, and require HTTP 200 health on both 8080 and 8091. A run is not
accepted if restoration or either health check fails. Preserve raw request,
trial, resource, and service artifacts under `/srv/ai`.

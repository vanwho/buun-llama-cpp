# 14-02 handoff - dynamic context, MTP, capacity, and teardown ladder

## Outcome

The real-model CUDA ladder is captured in:

- [DYNAMIC_CAPACITY_LADDER.md](../evidence/DYNAMIC_CAPACITY_LADDER.md)
- [DYNAMIC_CAPACITY_LADDER.json](../evidence/DYNAMIC_CAPACITY_LADDER.json)
- raw evidence: `/srv/ai/paged-kv/results/14-02-ladder-20260904T004547Z`

Auto-fit passed at 256, 16384, 32768, 65536, and 131072 resolved tokens.
The 262144 GGUF maximum refused before listening when VRAM demand could not be
met. The 65537 odd request resolved to 65792 and passed. Fixed hot-page,
headroom, and too-small-budget cases behaved deterministically, and native MTP
reported rows equal to resolved context with Turbo4 K/V on the GPU for the
passing MTP cases.

The isolated processes returned to the 148 MiB GPU baseline. The primary
profile, `llama-server.service`, `ai-long-memory.service`, and temporary
environment were restored; both health checks passed. The unrelated 8092 CPU
server was left untouched.

## Deferred verification

No required hardware, credential, or human-upstream verification is deferred.
The evidence records two runtime boundaries without overstating them: native
MTP is reserved after pager construction and therefore appears in separate
authoritative GPU residency lines, while the one-token ladder request did not
seal a full page and did not emit host catalog gauges in the captured metrics.

evidence claims bounded pager-target scaling, not replacement of that ordinary
allocation.

## Invariants

Requested, admitted, allocated, valid, and selected values have distinct names
and units. Target allocation bytes are realized backend allocation; target and
host valid bytes are derived from committed residency rows. Zero measurements
remain zero, while absent measurements remain absent. Queue/copy/wait fields are
microseconds, and transfer useful/aligned fields remain bytes. Acceptance has an
explicit predicted-token denominator. Snapshot generations and monotonic time
prevent combining counter deltas across request/config/reset boundaries.

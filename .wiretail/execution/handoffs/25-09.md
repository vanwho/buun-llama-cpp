## Invariants/evidence

The fixture covers GQA 24:4, permuted physical pages, native-position causal
masking, a masked row, one-row/17-row tails, page mass, serial partial state,
and split-KV merge. Decode-shaped default timings were Q=1 0.517 ms, Q=2
0.532 ms, Q=3 0.605 ms, Q=5 1.066 ms. At Q=64, explicit tile 16 was 3.003
ms versus 4.732 ms for tile 32 and 8.880 ms for tile 64. No whole-model or
native-MTP server throughput claim is made from this fixture.

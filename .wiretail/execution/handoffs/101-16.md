## Decisions and invariants

- The first changed owner was the f16 K/V dequant scratch VMM backing address:
  `kv_dequant_scratch_try` replaced an in-use pool during graph capture, and
  normal pool retirement called `cudaDeviceSynchronize()` inside capture.
- Preserve the synchronization for in-use pool retirement. During capture,
  retain replaced pools until capture ends, then retire them before returning.
  Discard an unpublished failed candidate without a device-wide wait.
- Graph inputs keep stable device pointers/capacities. Same-stream sideband
  copies precede graph reads; host data remains live until each completion.
  Keep separate captured shapes for Q1/Q2/Q3/Q16.
- No new graph cache or unsafe forced capture was introduced.

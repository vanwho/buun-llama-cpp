  remain structural. Selected content, page mapping, valid extent, and native
  masks refresh as mutable data.
- Existing packed-cache submit/completion leases preserve old owners through
  stream completion; hybrid reuse retains recurrent shape/head/binding epoch
  checks after shared attention validation.
- Rebuild reason accounting is gated by exact `LLAMA_HOTPATH_PROFILE=1`.

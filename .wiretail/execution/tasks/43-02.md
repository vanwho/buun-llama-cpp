## Required identity, measurements, and invariant

Use CUDA selected-packed attention with target/draft K/V Turbo4, GPU draft,
native MTP, `L8192/H4096/A2048/B128/U64`, and 256-token pages. Run focused
tests, then one natural incremental C>H recall/append fixture. Eligibility
requires a non-sentinel current-Q candidate, `attention_samples > 0`,
published promotion, useful H2D bytes, `selected_cold_pages_used > 0`, and
coherent committed recall/append. Forced transfer is not natural proof.

Repeat the original q0/q1/q2 prompts with fresh slots and matched C6144,
128-committed-token selected-packed, explicit CPU-main-KV/GPU-native-MTP, and
pager-off all-GPU profiles. Record per-prompt prefill/decode, TTFT, MTP
proposed/accepted, memory categories, ratios, and nulls for unmeasured
claims. Preserve the distinction between active up-to-128K and overall 256K.

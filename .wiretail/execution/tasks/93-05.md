# 93-05 — Produce the MTP diagnosis and schedule only necessary follow-up

Revision: `mtp-diagnostic-v10-20260922`. Amendment: `repair93-mtp-fast-20260922`.

Cluster: `repair93-mtp-diagnostics-v10`.

## Objective

Turn the short diagnostic evidence into an actionable conclusion before any
large-context or speed campaign resumes.

## Work

1. Review only the compact outputs from `93-01` through `93-04` and their raw
   hashes. Do not reopen every historical phase or repeat old frontier runs.
2. State separately whether: GPU Turbo4 MTP placement works; dense MTP
   acceptance works; resident paged MTP matches dense control; cold-page
   promotion works; and the automatic route is fast-native.
3. If any item fails, create the smallest ordered successor task(s) with exact
   source file/function, invariant, focused test, and bounded command. Do not
   schedule a 256K or speed campaign until the failing short control passes.
4. If all short controls pass, leave a handoff authorizing the later phase to
   perform context scaling and speed measurements; include the exact binary,
   model, cache types, geometry, and route policy to reuse.

## Acceptance

The review has one unambiguous MTP verdict and no “deferred” wording for a
required short control. Any unresolved defect becomes a concrete successor,
not another evidence-only loop.

## Deliverable

Create `handoffs/93-05.md`, `evidence/V10_93-05.json/.md`, and
`evidence/V10_REPAIR93_MTP_REVIEW.json` with the verdict and successor task
IDs, if any.

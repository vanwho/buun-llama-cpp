## Objective

Repair the measured phase-57 full-L first-request invalid-response boundary
before scheduling another occupancy or speed campaign. Keep target KV Turbo4
on CUDA and full-L draft KV Turbo4 on GPU.

## Exact owners and hypothesis

Inspect the existing owners `llama_kv_cache::vbr_scratch_reserve`,
`ggml_backend_cuda_kv_dequant_scratch_memory`,
`llama_kv_attention_packed_cache::find_or_create`,
`llama_kv_attention_packed_cache::submit_graph`, and CUDA
`launch_mul_mat_q`. Hypothesis: one combined VBR scratch, packed-attention,
dynamic-shared-memory, graph, or pager-write reservation still fails before a
full-L request can advance its occupied frontier, or leaves ownership work
pending after refusal.

## Minimal failing fixture, invariant, and numeric discriminator

Run both smallest measured boundaries: `L262144/H30208/B128/U128` with the
1200-token request, and `L262144/H16384/B128/U64` with the first request.
Before is phase-57 `occupied_C262144.status=failed`, invalid response, and
`C_tokens=0`. After the focused regression must show every scratch/packed/
launch reservation charged before graph submission, zero pending pager writes
after refusal, and one successful request with observed `C >= 1200` while
preserving the required Turbo4/GPU placements; graph replay must not grow the
reserved scratch unexpectedly.

## Stop condition and proof

Stop at the first accounting, launch-resource, cleanup, identity, or placement
mismatch. Do not lower `L`, move draft KV to CPU, switch attention routes, or
start an occupancy curve to hide the failure. Required proof:
`full_l_first_request_boundary_repair`.

## Exact defect and scope

In ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fattn.cu (~4352), the fused guard combines !turbo_kv
with conditions that necessarily require turbo_kv. Decode/verification of
contiguous packed Turbo4 falls through to full-range GPU F16 materialization.
The disabling guard is branch regression ae02b1bb70, not origin/master behavior.
Fix only matched Turbo4 K/V at supported D128/256 and Q1–4 first. Leave mixed
tiers on their existing numerically proven routes; no blanket guard removal.

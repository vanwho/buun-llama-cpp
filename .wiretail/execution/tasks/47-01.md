## Fixture, before/after test, and stop condition

Before the change, run the focused telemetry, execution, page-select, and
model-free pager tests; the phase-45 receipt is the live baseline with 26
current-Q samples and zero candidate/promotion/use. After the change, run the
same focused tests plus the smallest available CUDA C>H natural recall/append
smoke, asserting explicit candidate, promotion, submitted/useful H2D,
selected-cold-use, identity, and coherent-output counters.

Stop if the first production edge remains absent or its drop/readiness cause
cannot be identified: record the exact owning symbol and reason, keep the
route fail-closed, and do not use forced transfer or a synthetic candidate as
proof.

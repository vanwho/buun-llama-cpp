# 17-09 — Produce phase-17 performance benchmark evidence

## Objective

Measure whether hot Turbo4 pager pages plus fully GPU-resident Turbo4 MTP
achieve a material speed improvement over ordinary CPU-KV offload.

## Read

- Cluster `17d-benchmark-quality`, handoff 17-08, performance contract, and
  the canonical benchmark runner.

## Work

Pair the same prompts and resolved contexts across ordinary CPU-KV, selective
  Turbo4 pager with GPU MTP, exact/reference, observe, and safe all-GPU control
  where feasible. Use at least ten warm trials plus cold-needle, focus-shift,
  churn, and feature-off segments. Record prompt/decode throughput, TTFT,
  p50/p95/p99, MTP acceptance, VRAM/RAM, faults, H2D/D2H, queue/copy/wait,
  prefetch, overlap, and rollback errors. Discover hot capacity from the
  ledger; never encode or report a fixed hot-token count as a production rule.

## Acceptance

All comparisons use one binary/model/corpus and have raw records. Report the
3x floor and 5x/70% targets honestly, including null ratios when a paired
segment is unavailable. Preserve failed hypotheses for remediation.

## Deliverable

Create `handoffs/17-09.md`, `PHASE17_PERFORMANCE.json/.md`, and raw results.

## Measured finding and exact hypothesis

The selected Stage 1 rows in `SHORT_PATH_POSTMERGE.json` completed with fresh
prefill 210.54/210.61/210.51 tok/s on 4329/4326/4327 fresh tokens, below the
500 tok/s floor. Request journals record five live-rewind checkpoint images
per 4.3K-token prompt, around 151–154 MiB each. Near the end, four images are
created at frontiers less than 300 tokens apart. The single-request prefill
timing is 20.54–20.56 seconds; it does not isolate checkpoint wall time.

Hypothesis: the completion loop in `tools/server/server-context.cpp`, around
`live_rewind_checkpoint`, `checkpoint_capacity`, `do_checkpoint`, and staging
before publication, captures and replaces redundant near-end live-rewind
images. Those captures may materially inflate fresh prefill. Coalesce a
near-end image before snapshot construction when it cannot serve a distinct
MTP rollback or final-user replay frontier. Keep a valid recent rewind image,
the final-user/query seam, cancellation state, and the MTP reject/rollback
frontier. Do not relax Turbo4, MTP, the 500 tok/s goal, or H admission.

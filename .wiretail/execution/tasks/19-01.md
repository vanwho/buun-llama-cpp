## Ordered implementation / experiment

1. Trace physical allocation ownership from context construction to pager initialization. Identify logical tensor dimensions, committed GPU buffers, aliases, pager slab and native-MTP compute separately; produce the before allocation ledger.

2. Move pager admission/planning early enough that full logical target context does not first allocate full physical GPU target KV. Keep logical cell metadata in host memory; create bounded GPU target storage and page-aware views. Off/observe retain existing semantics.

3. Reserve full dynamic MTP K/V and measured/worst-case MTP compute, target compute/ubatch shapes, recurrent state, staging, summaries, descriptors, checkpoint/rollback and safety margin before hot slots. Count shared buffers once.

4. Compute safe hot capacity from device/backend occupancy and aligned page charges with checked arithmetic. Reconcile projected and post-warmup measured high-water memory; expose rejection reasons by category.

5. At fixed target/MTP context, reduce hot slots or workspace/ubatch on near-OOM. Never reduce MTP rows, swap codec, disable draft, or hide a full target KV allocation behind a view.

6. Remove accidental 1024-page or reference-layer-count global limits where not architectural; preserve explicit supported-model capability checks. Large logical capacity must not resize scratch by full context.

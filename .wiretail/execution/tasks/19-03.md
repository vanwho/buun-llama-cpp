## Ordered implementation / experiment

1. Trace production call sites for policy and transaction APIs. Wire a real decode/ubatch boundary with current sequence/generation, admitted residency pool, backend transport and completion hooks; pure unit calls are insufficient.

2. Start with deterministic manually selected logical page IDs and synchronous completion at a safe boundary. Separate this diagnostic policy from the later attention-aware policy.

3. Choose occupied history exceeding a deliberately small test hot budget. Seal a page, evict it, promote it from host, wait on its actual CUDA event, and publish the completed slot only after identity revalidation.

4. Ensure residency tensor consumed by attention contains the promoted bytes, not a separately retained dense cache. Correlate per-layer page IDs/slot, host checksum, upload checksum, copy event and attention use.

5. Propagate backpressure/map/copy failures to clear retry/refusal paths. Charge copy counters at actual submission/completion, not policy intent.

6. Show a genuine old-page retrieval answer after promotion, and a warm repetition with no redundant upload; don't infer physical movement from graph epochs.

# 17-08 — Produce phase-17 quality benchmark evidence

## Objective

Run the model-backed quality campaign needed by the later final assessment,
using one fixed Qwen3.8-27B UD-IQ4_XS provenance and the repaired corpus/runtime.

## Read

- Cluster `17d-benchmark-quality`, handoffs 17-01 through 17-07, benchmark
  contract, corpus manifest, and quality acceptance thresholds.

## Work

Run, in order, dense control, selected-all reference, exact, and selective
attention with native GPU Turbo4 MTP where requested. Cover calibration and
held-out cases, resolved dynamic contexts, cold needles, tails, focus shifts,
and churn. Capture output/logit parity, exact score, routing recall, attention
mass, MTP placement/acceptance, pager residency/transfer telemetry, errors,
and declared ablations. A dense-control failure stops the quality gate; never
attribute it to paging or change the checker after observing output.

Capacity-failure recovery is mandatory. The historical 77,824-token probe is
only a diagnostic input and is not a required production context or a reason
to block the campaign by itself. If native-MTP startup reports CUDA allocation
failure or low free VRAM, stop the failed launcher through the established
benchmark/profile harness using non-interactive `sudo -n`, record the exact
failure, and immediately retry the same model/build/configuration at the next
smaller context in the declared ladder (for example 65,536, 49,152, 32,768,
24,576, then 16,384, adjusted to the configured context contract). Continue
until a feasible native-MTP point is measured or the complete ladder is
exhausted. Do not mark the quality gate blocked after one oversized context,
and do not substitute a CPU-only run for the required CUDA evidence. A block
is justified only when every contract-valid ladder point fails or a distinct
unsupported-geometry error is proven.

## Acceptance

Every run has matching model/tokenizer/binary/config/corpus hashes, raw
machine-readable records, and a clear pass/block decision. The task may report
quality failure, but may not declare the overall project complete.

## Deliverable

Create `handoffs/17-08.md`, `PHASE17_QUALITY.json/.md`, and raw results under
the configured benchmark results directory.

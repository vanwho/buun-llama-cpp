## Required symbols and invariant

Work around `llm_graph_context::build_attn_inp_kv`,
`llama_context::publish_kv_attention_telemetry`, and
`llama_kv_attention_telemetry::publish_completed`, plus their direct test
seams. For every completed eligible selected-packed query, the same valid
snapshot/query/page identity must reach one publication attempt; a rejected
event must increment its precise drop counter. The natural proof invariant is
`attention_samples > 0` plus a non-sentinel candidate, without changing
`selected <= A_by_layer <= H <= L` or the Turbo4 target/draft GPU-native-MTP
identity.

## Fixture, before/after test, and stop condition

Use the focused telemetry fixture to reproduce the zero-publication assertion,
then the smallest C>H incremental natural-recall fixture. Before the change,
capture the current invocation/drop counters; after it, run
`test-kv-attention-telemetry` and the available model-free pager checks, then
the bounded natural smoke if the CUDA build is available. Stop without scale
if the smoke still has zero samples, no non-sentinel candidate, or no useful
H2D; record the exact first missing boundary rather than broadening the fix.

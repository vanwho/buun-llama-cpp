Revision: `hotpath-v10-20260914`. Cluster: `57b-bundle-identity-v10`.

`tools/server/bench/run-final-curve.py::_runtime_identity`, `main`, and
`_write_receipt`, together with the existing
`run-pager-profile-benchmark.py::write_bundle_manifest` and
`bundle_identity_errors` checks. A measured campaign must receive an explicit
`--server-bin`/`BENCH_SERVER_BIN` candidate, write the immutable build receipt
and bundle manifest, and retain the endpoint PID's executable/DSO hashes and

candidate, its project DSO, and an immutable build receipt, then exercise the
identity path once with the candidate and once without it or with one loaded
hash changed. The invariant is that the matching case records a non-null
manifest SHA and all loaded project files are in the manifest; missing or
mismatched identity fails closed and cannot be serialized as a measured row.

Numeric before/after test: before, `V10_SUMMARY_55.json` has
`source_identity.bundle_manifest.status=not_recorded`; after, the harness
must produce one manifest SHA, one observed executable hash, and at least one
matching project-DSO hash for the same endpoint PID, while the mismatch
fixture produces a nonzero validation result.

Stop at the first executable/DSO/model mismatch or writable-bundle violation;
retain the identity diagnostic and do not compare speed modes with mixed
provenance.

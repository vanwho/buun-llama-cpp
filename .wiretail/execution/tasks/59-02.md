prefix/token/template state. Run the bounded T1 controlled model-query edge
and one organic T2 or T3 round trip with answer correctness recorded as a

and 128K pilots only after the small proof and stop conditions permit them.

The invariant is that identity and model/workload hashes match, every native
row has `draft_delta > 0` and `0 <= accepted_delta <= draft_delta`, cached
append has observed cache reuse, physical promotion requires completed H2D,
published mapping, and completed target-graph use, and answer quality is not
inferred from physical use. The numeric before state is phase-57's three
native rows, q0-only controls, zero cached-append samples, no promotion or
quality rows, and full-L `C=0`. The after state is a complete 27-row matched
matrix with real denominators, both append probes, mechanically complete T1
and organic evidence or a truthful policy result, separate quality rows, and
`max_observed_C >= 262144` only if that boundary truly completes.

reason; do not import phase-55/57 rates or infer C from launcher arguments.

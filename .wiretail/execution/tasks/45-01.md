## Required symbols and invariant

Audit and, where necessary, repair the bridge owned by
`llama_kv_attention_telemetry::publish_completed`,
`llama_kv_cache::capture_kv_routing_query`,
`llama_kv_cache::apply_pager_live_policy`, and
`llama_kv_prefetch_mailbox::poll`/`take_ready`. For each eligible completed
selected-packed query, the candidate and subsequent promotion must retain the
same table epoch, query generation, page generation, sequence identity, and
page identity. The invariant remains `selected <= A_by_layer <= H <= L`, with
target/draft Turbo4 K/V, GPU draft, native MTP, and full-L production routing.

not turn telemetry into fabricated selection evidence.

## Fixture, before/after test, and stop condition

`test-kv-attention-execution`, `test-kv-page-select`, and the available
model-free pager checks; the phase-43 natural receipt is the live baseline
with 36 current-Q samples and zero candidate/promotion/use. After the change,
run the same focused fixtures and the smallest available CUDA C>H natural

boundary if the first production edge remains absent; report its symbol and

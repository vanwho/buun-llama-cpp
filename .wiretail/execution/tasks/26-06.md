Table-content updates can use stable device buffers when safe; actual layout/shape/address changes require the correct recapture. Counters must reflect CUDA capture/replay, not merely frontend graph submission.

Ensure each committed hidden row is consumed once, pending/rejected rows retain correct offset, and no mandatory whole hidden-row D2H/H2D roundtrip remains. Keep full-L draft KV. Tune in portable code; machine affinity/power/pinned allocation settings remain site-only and measured under load, not idle PCIe snapshots.
8. Apply at most one identified bottleneck change at a time, rerun one primary sample, and keep only helpful or necessary corrections. Negative experiments complete their evaluation with evidence; no need to force a change in every category.

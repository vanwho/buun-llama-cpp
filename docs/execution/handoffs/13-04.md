# 13-04 handoff — prefill, graph reuse, scratch, and allocation churn

## Outcome

Retained three bounded graph-input optimizations. M-RoPE and attention-
temperature host staging now uses grow-only buffers owned by the graph input,
removing a temporary host allocation from every submission. Selected
reference/direct descriptors and masks are immutable graph inputs: they are
prepared once when a graph is captured and reused unchanged on replay. Cache
indices and telemetry state remain dynamic and are refreshed for every
submission.

The three tested hypotheses were: H1, grow-only host staging; H2, capture-only
selected descriptors; and H3, capture-only selected-mask preparation with a
tree-mask refresh exception. All were retained because the deterministic
contract does not change tensor shape, route qualification, values, or
ownership.

The direct execution metric now reports actual page-table upload work: the
unchanged replay in the deterministic graph-key sequence is zero-upload. The
two-page fixture therefore reports 48 bytes rather than counting the same
48-byte descriptor twice. The admission path remains physical-row bounded:
`min(configured_ubatch, physical_page_count * page_tokens)`; scheduler compute
buffer sizing is measured before pager initialization and is charged once as
the combined graph/dequant scratch reservation. No fixed hot page count or
logical-context-sized GPU allocation was added.

## Profiles and sweeps

The required short/medium/long-over-`H`, odd-tail, checkpoint-restore, and
first-decode model profiles were inspected in the frozen phase-13 shape
matrix. They cannot be executed in this checkout because the external service
does not expose a current-tree selected pager route with component counters.
The deterministic admission sweep covers configured 4096 rows with two
physical pages, alternate 128-row page geometry, zero physical pages, and a
zero configured ubatch. The odd-tail and prefill-to-decode admission contracts
remain covered by `test-kv-attention-execution`.

The before/after deterministic accounting is preserved in
[`prefill-graph-profile-13-04.txt`](../evidence/prefill-graph-profile-13-04.txt).
It records five post-change trials and the descriptor accounting delta
(`96 -> 48` bytes). It deliberately makes no pp, TTFT, decode-throughput,
VRAM, or quality claim.

## Invariants/evidence

The direct CUDA fixture covers T4/T4, D=256, GQA=4, one-page tails, a
permuted physical layout with a logical gap, native-position causal filtering,
compact masking, expected-value comparison, score reduction on/off, and
fail-closed type/shape validation. The resource audit confirms no local or
static shared memory in the direct decoder and no F16 full-cache allocation in
the fixture. CPU fake-backend and deterministic lifecycle rows are linked
without being misreported as live CUDA transfer results.

## Deferred verification

The required GPU was available and bounded hardware checks passed. Live
Qwen3.8 model-graph comparisons, 304-page pressure, dense/gather/paged full
graph parity, prefill/multi-sequence behavior, CUDA transfer fault injection,
native MTP/recurrent teardown, and CUDA graph replay with real table epochs
require the external checkpoint and later integration harness. They remain
explicitly deferred and are not claimed as passes.

## Remaining risks

The direct kernel is intentionally fail-closed to one supported decode shape.
Its bounded fixture does not prove full model graph correctness or production
CUDA transfer ownership. No tolerance was widened and no assertion was
disabled to obtain the reported passes.
